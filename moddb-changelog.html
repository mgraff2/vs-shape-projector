Jonastech Shape Projector 1.0.0 — for Vintage Story 1.22.x

  • First release. A craftable block that casts building guides onto the world in light. Place it, right-click it, dial in a figure. It places nothing and breaks nothing — it only shows you where.
  • Six shapes: circle, ring, ellipse, rectangle, regular polygon (three sides being the triangle) and Archimedean spiral. A ring draws both of its circles and leaves the band between them empty, which is the moat you dig.
  • Up to forty-eight layers around one shared centre, so concentric is the default state rather than something you achieve. The centre moves in half-block steps — on a block for an odd diameter, on the seam for an even one — and may sit well away from the projector, so the instrument stands beside the work rather than in the middle of it.
  • Thickness per layer. Mark a figure several blocks broad in one layer instead of stacking several a block apart. The band grows inward, so the outer edge stays exactly where the radius puts it.
  • Height per layer. Stand a figure several blocks tall in one layer, so a thirty-block round tower is one layer rather than thirty. In Follow terrain each column measures from its own ground, so a wall of even height rises over uneven land.
  • Follow terrain. The outline lies on the surface like a rope laid out by hand and follows the floor down as you excavate, so a dig line is never lost halfway through the digging. Water is yours to rule per layer: rest on the surface and mark the bank, or look through to the bed and mark the bottom.
  • Fixed height for the level work — wall tops, floors, platforms. Where the earth swallows the line, cut down to it; where it hangs in air, fill up to it.
  • Marks turn green as you fill them, so the figure completes in front of you and what is left is obvious at a glance. Every level of a tall layer is checked separately, so a half-built wall greens as far up as it is finished.
  • A holographic miniature of the whole design floats above the block, world-aligned so north in the mini is north in the world. Read a forty-layer plan without walking it. Switchable on its own without stopping the projection.
  • Named presets, saved per player rather than per projector, so a shape worked out once follows you to every projector you can edit. Load one in place of what is there, or add it on top.
  • Shortcuts for the repetitive part: Page Up copies the selected layer a course higher, Page Down copies it a block outward toward a filled disc, and + / − resize every layer at once — hidden layers included, so nothing drifts out of step. A layer that would shrink past its minimum is left alone and says so.
  • Every control explains itself. Hovering a button tells you what it will do to the layer you have selected, with the arithmetic worked out — "radius 11 → 12", "Y offset +2 → +3" — and a greyed-out button says why it is greyed out.
  • O hides every projection for you alone, rebindable; other players still see theirs.
  • A configured projector keeps its layers when broken and replaced, or carried with Carry On.
  • Server-authoritative. Radius, layer count, thickness, height, the guide-block budget, render distance and the miniature are all set in ModConfig/shapeprojector.json; a client cannot ask for a figure the server has not agreed to.
  • Tested against Vintage Story 1.22.0 through 1.22.7, each patch booted clean on its own and alongside Carry On, Chisel Tools, Farseer and Claims Radar.
